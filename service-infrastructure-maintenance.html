What’s included

01

Proactive monitoring

We watch the health of your servers, network devices and storage continuously, with alerts that reach an engineer before a warning becomes an outage.

  • Performance, capacity and availability metrics
  • Alert thresholds tuned to your environment
  • Regular health reports
02

Patch management

Operating system and firmware updates tested and applied on a schedule that fits your business, closing security gaps without unplanned downtime.

03

Hardware lifecycle management

An up-to-date inventory of your hardware with warranty and end-of-support dates, so replacements are planned and budgeted — not emergencies.

04

Backup & recovery

On-premises and cloud backups configured, monitored and — most importantly — restore-tested, so you know your data can actually come back.

Technical support

A team that already knows your environment, for incidents, changes and day-to-day questions.

FAQ

Questions we hear about this

Something else on your mind? Ask an engineer.

Do you support equipment you didn’t install?

Yes. We start by reviewing and documenting what’s there, and flag anything that’s out of support or at risk.

Is support on-site or remote?

Both. Monitoring and most patching are done remotely; hardware work is done on-site.

Can you look after systems in AWS too?

Yes. The same team looks after on-premises and AWS, so problems that cross both are handled once.
