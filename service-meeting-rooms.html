Workplace systems

Meeting room solutions

We turn meeting rooms into reliable collaboration spaces, with audio-visual and conferencing technology that’s simple for everyone to use.

Upgrade a meeting room

What’s included

A complete room

Every device placed for how people actually meet

We design the room as one system — sightlines, acoustics, cabling and controls — then install it, test it and hand it over with documentation.

Audio-visual integration

High-definition displays, 4K projectors, ceiling speakers and wireless presentation that lets anyone share their screen with one click.

Video conferencing

Certified hardware for Microsoft Teams and Zoom Rooms: speaker-tracking cameras and microphone arrays that capture every voice clearly.

Room control & automation

One touch panel for lighting, shades, displays, audio and calls — so meetings start on time instead of with a hunt for remotes.

Room scheduling panels

Digital panels outside each room show real-time availability and allow booking on the spot, synced with Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace calendars.
