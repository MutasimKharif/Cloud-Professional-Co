Data centers

Data center design & build

We design, build and maintain mission-critical data center environments with a focus on reliability, efficiency and security — every system, from the floor tiles up.

Discuss your facility

The whole lifecycle

The heart of your IT operations

A data center is only as reliable as its weakest system. We handle every part of the facility so power, cooling, fire protection and monitoring are designed to work together.

What’s included

01

Data center build

We manage greenfield projects from the ground up — site selection, architectural design and construction — so the finished facility meets industry best practice and your operational requirements.

  • Raised floors, cabling and containment
  • Rack layout and capacity planning
  • Handover documentation
02

Environmental monitoring system (EMS)

Sensors for temperature, humidity, airflow, water leaks and power usage, with real-time alerts that stop environmental problems before they cause downtime.

03

Precision cooling

High-density computing needs specialized cooling. We design and install CRAC and CRAH units that hold temperature and humidity precisely, for performance and a longer life for your servers.

04

Fire suppression

Clean-agent systems such as FM-200 and Novec 1230 that put out fires quickly without damaging sensitive electronics, protecting both equipment and business continuity.

05

UPS & power

Uninterruptible power supplies, generators and power distribution units sized and configured to deliver clean, consistent power and ride through outages.
