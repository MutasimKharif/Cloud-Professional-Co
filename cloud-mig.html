AWS cloud

Cloud migration & strategy

A strategic path to AWS: we assess what you run, choose the right approach for each application, and move it with minimal disruption to your business.

Plan your migration

What’s included

01

Cloud readiness assessment

We analyze your infrastructure, applications and their dependencies to build a detailed migration roadmap — including total cost of ownership and a risk assessment you can take to management.

  • Application and dependency inventory
  • TCO comparison: on-premises vs. AWS
  • Phased roadmap with risks and owners
02

Secure data migration

We transfer critical data to AWS securely and efficiently using services such as AWS Database Migration Service, keeping systems in sync so the final cut-over window is as short as possible.

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Continuous replication until cut-over
  • Validation and rollback plan

Migration paths

The right move for each application

Not everything should be rebuilt, and not everything should be lifted as-is. The assessment decides, application by application.

“Lift and shift”

Rehost

Move applications to AWS with minimal changes. The fastest route to leaving ageing hardware, and immediate infrastructure savings.

“Lift and reshape”

Replatform

Move while making targeted cloud optimizations — for example, managed databases on Amazon RDS — for better performance and resilience.

Cloud-native

Refactor

Rebuild applications as cloud-native services — serverless or microservices — where the business case justifies maximum scalability and agility.

After go-live

Post-migration optimization

Once you’re running on AWS, we tune the environment for cost, performance and security — right-sizing, Savings Plans and storage tiers — so you get the most out of your investment.
