About CloudPros

Cloud transformation, built in Riyadh

We are more than a service provider: we are a partner for organizations navigating cloud, network and data center decisions.

Our story

Closing the gap between potential and adoption

CloudPros was founded in Riyadh by a team of AWS-certified engineers who saw a clear gap: the cloud’s potential was enormous, but many businesses in the region weren’t getting the benefit. We set out to be a true partner in that transformation, not just another vendor.

From our first migration to our work today across cloud strategy, DevOps, security, networking and data centers, the commitment has stayed the same: give regional organizations the world-class tools and expertise they need to compete globally.

Mission

To design, build and manage secure, scalable and cost-effective solutions that deliver real business value and accelerate innovation for our clients.

Vision

To be the leading catalyst for digital transformation in the Middle East, known for technical excellence, integrity and genuine partnership.

FAQ

Questions we hear often

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What makes CloudPros different from other cloud consultants?

We cover the whole stack — AWS, the network and the physical data center — with one team, and we work as an extension of yours. You get certified expertise, designs tailored to your business and transparent communication, not just a delivered project.

Which industries do you work with?

We have experience across FinTech, e-commerce, government and enterprise IT. The principles we build on — security, scalability and compliance — apply to any organization moving to the cloud.

How do you keep my data and infrastructure secure?

Security is designed in from the start. We follow the AWS Well-Architected Framework with defense in depth: strict IAM policies, network segmentation, encryption at rest and in transit, and continuous threat monitoring. We also help clients meet standards such as ISO 27001 and SOC 2.
