About CloudPros

Cloud transformation, built in Riyadh

An engineering firm in Riyadh for the parts of IT that have to work together: AWS, the network and the data center.

Our story

Closing the gap between potential and adoption

CloudPros was founded in Riyadh by a team of AWS-certified engineers who saw a clear gap: the cloud’s potential was enormous, but many businesses in the region weren’t getting the benefit. We set out to be a true partner in that transformation, not just another vendor.

From our first migration to our work today across cloud strategy, DevOps, security, networking and data centers, the commitment has stayed the same: give regional organizations the world-class tools and expertise they need to compete globally.

Mission

Design, build and run infrastructure that organizations in the Kingdom can rely on and understand — from the AWS account to the server room.

Vision

To be the first team organizations in the Kingdom call for anything between their users and the rack.

FAQ

Questions we hear often

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What makes CloudPros different from other cloud consultants?

We cover the whole stack — AWS, the network and the physical data center — with one team, and we work as an extension of yours. Problems that cross layers have one owner, and everything we build is documented and handed to your people.

Which industries do you work with?

We have experience across FinTech, e-commerce, government and enterprise IT. The principles we build on — security, resilience and compliance with Saudi regulation — apply to any organization moving to the cloud.

How do you keep my data and infrastructure secure?

Security is designed in from the start. We follow the AWS Well-Architected Framework with defense in depth: strict IAM policies, network segmentation, encryption at rest and in transit, and continuous threat monitoring. We map controls to the NCA’s Essential and Cloud Cybersecurity Controls — and to SAMA’s framework or the PDPL where they apply — and to ISO 27001.
